## Parameter Estimation

Assume you have a coin, but you don’t know if it’s a fair coin, which means, the probability of head when you toss it is not 50%.

Let’s say $p = P(Head)$. If it’s a fair coin,  $p$ = 0.5.

But the fact is you don’t know $p$, so you have an experiment, you toss the coin 10 times, and the result is: H, H, T, H, T. There are 3 heads and 2 tails. Based on the result, what do you think the most possible $p$ is? Naturally, we think $p$ is 0.6.

This is parameter estimation, we don't know the real parameter $p$, but we observe the result, and hope to estimate $p$.

## Maximum Likelihood Estimation


$$
\hat{\theta}
=
\arg\min_\theta L(\theta)
$$

$\theta$: model parameter 
$L$: loss function / objective function
$min$: the minimum of $L$
$arg\ min$: when $L$ is minimum, what's the corresponding argument($\theta$)

Define:

$$
\hat{\theta}_{MLE}
=
\arg\max_\theta p(D\mid\theta)
$$
